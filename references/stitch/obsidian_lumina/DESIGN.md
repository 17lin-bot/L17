## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an avant-garde digital gallery aesthetic tailored for world-class 2D/3D artists, art directors, and creative technologists. It bridges museum-grade restraint with hyper-modern digital kinetic energy. The identity projects uncompromising craftsmanship, visionary technical depth, and luxury-tier exclusivity.

### Aesthetic Paradigm
- **Dark Matter Minimalism:** Expansive, atmospheric noir backdrops provide negative space so that high-fidelity renders, interactive WebGL viewports, and kinetic typography take absolute center stage.
- **Luminescent Glassmorphism:** Micro-surfaces employ deep translucent obsidian overlays with hairline refractive edges that mimic optic crystal and titanium chassis.
- **Electric Precision:** High-chroma ultraviolet and laser cyan accents are treated not as decorative fill, but as directed photonic emitters—used strictly for focal guidance, active state illumination, and reactive feedback loops.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high dynamic range optical contrast. Primary illumination cuts through zero-luminance obsidian grounds with surgical clarity.

### Functional Palette
- **Canvas Base (`#0A0A0C`):** The infinite void; baseline background layer for global viewports.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#121216`):** Charcoal obsidian container surface for structural panels, drawers, and modal backdrops.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#1B1B22`):** Interactive card planes and floating toolbars.
- **Primary Beam (`#00F0FF`):** Electric cyan used for primary interactive highlights, focused states, cursor light sources, and active telemetry.
- **Secondary Aura (`#8A2BE2`):** Iridescent ultraviolet utilized for ambient back-glows, luxury badges, generative art gradients, and high-impact metadata tags.
- **Lustrous Platinum (`#F3F4F6`):** Dominant editorial type, high-contrast indicators, and pure display headlines.
- **Muted Platinum (`#9CA3AF`):** Secondary editorial, technical metadata, and inactive controls.
- **Hairline Border Spectrum:** 8% to 15% opacity white or subtle cyan-to-violet linear gradients against pitch black surfaces.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the sculpted structural forms of `Syne` for titles and display anchors with the calculated, technical cadence of `Space Grotesk` for reading sequences and navigation.

- **Display & Headings:** Rendered in `Syne` with deliberate character tracking. For massive hero banners, tracking tightens slightly to create dense, impactful sculptural blocks. Section eyebrow headers leverage `label-caps` with extreme letter spacing to impart architectural rhythm.
- **Body & Annotations:** Set in `Space Grotesk` to deliver crisp legibility against illuminated black glass surfaces. Line heights are calibrated generously to prevent ocular strain in low-light environments.
- **Numbers & Metadata:** Always set in `label-mono` with tabular figures enabled for project coordinates, polycounts, software tags, and timestamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts an editorial fluid grid governed by generous framing margins, allowing visual artwork to exist without claustrophobic boundary clipping.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12-column fluid grid. Outer section margin fixed at `4rem` (`margin-desktop`) with column gutters at `2.5rem` (`gutter-desktop`). Asymmetric layout modules allow artwork to claim 7 to 9 columns while technical project statements claim the remainder.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column fluid grid with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Staggered vertical cards collapse into synchronized multi-level pairs.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4-column fluid grid with `1.25rem` margins (`margin`) and `1rem` gutters. Elements reflow strictly into dynamic single-column ribbons.

### Spacing Architecture
Negative space is treated as physical mass. Layout components prioritize macro-separation (`space-xl` and higher) between disparate project case studies, while internal component metadata clusters tightly (`space-xs` through `space-md`) to enforce strong Gestalt grouping.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through optical optics: refractive transparency, dark tinted back-panels, and directional chromatic backlighting. Traditional muddy drop-shadows are strictly prohibited.

### Elevation Architecture
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Pure base obsidian (`#0A0A0C`), absorption layer.
- **Layer 1 (Recessed Panels / Trays):** Semi-opaque `#121216` with a `1px` inner boundary of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Glassmorphic Cards):** `rgba(18, 18, 22, 0.65)` layered over hardware-accelerated `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(140%)`. Outlined with a top-weighted razor gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4) 0%, rgba(138, 43, 226, 0.2) 40%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05) 100%)`.
- **Layer 3 (Overlays, Flyouts & Modals):** `rgba(22, 22, 28, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`. Casts a diffused volumetric aura: `box-shadow: 0 24px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85), 0 0 32px 0 rgba(138, 43, 226, 0.15)`.
- **Interactive Luminescence:** When focused or hovered, cards and interactive nodes bleed a faint neon radiance (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25)`), creating the illusion of glowing phosphor tubes positioned behind edge-lit glass.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary emphasizes architectural rigor through restrained, subtle corner rounding. Large pill radii are rejected in favor of chiseled, calibrated contours that echo optical instrumentation and display hardware.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Controls, inputs, chips, and micro badges.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Medium artwork cards, navigation docks, and context menus.
- **Extra Large Radius (`rounded-xl`, 0.75rem / 12px):** Master viewport wrappers, modal dialogues, and immersive full-bleed preview enclosures.
- **Chamfer Overrides:** Special display containers may employ a symmetrical 45-degree corner clip (2px to 4px) to simulate precision-milled physical frames.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Cyber-Action:** Built on an electric cyan gradient with high-density black typography (`#0A0A0C`). On hover, transitions into a high-intensity cyan corona bloom with a 1px upward mechanical translation.
- **Secondary Ghost:** Smoked obsidian surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`) with a continuous hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) and platinum text. Hover state stimulates an iridescent border flare transitioning from ultraviolet to cyan.
- **Minimalist Icon Buttons:** Borderless `36x36px` frame; icons tinted `#9CA3AF`, shifting to `#00F0FF` with a subtle circular backlight bloom on hover.

### Glassmorphic Project Cards
- Crafted with translucent obsidian backdrops and a 1px composite refractive border.
- Media elements scale smoothly (`transform: scale(1.03)`) within an overflow-hidden mask upon hover.
- Metadata overlays slide in from the base using a cubic bezier timing function (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), illuminating the primary project category using electric cyan tracking labels.

### Badges & Filter Chips
- **Status & Technical Chips:** Compact height (`24px`), using uppercase `label-caps`. 
- **Inactive State:** Dark background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`), muted text (`#9CA3AF`), zero shadow.
- **Active State:** Deep violet base (`rgba(138, 43, 226, 0.2)`) encased in a glowing neon-cyan border (`#00F0FF`), with high-intensity platinum text.

### Form Inputs & Telemetry Fields
- Monolithic styling: `#121216` background with an inset 1px dark border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- Typography defaults to `Space Grotesk` (`body-sm`).
- Active focus state introduces a solid `1px` electric cyan perimeter accompanied by a faint external luminous spill (`0 0 12px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.35)`). Placeholder copy is muted (`#6B7280`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Crisp square (checkbox, 2px radius) and diamond-profile circular enclosures.
- Selected state fills with `#00F0FF`, bearing a centered deep-black glyph or core dot.

### Specialized Portfolio Components
- **Model Telemetry Bar:** Monospaced viewport strip showing mesh polygons, texture resolution (e.g., `8K UDIM`), shader tech, and lighting engines in `label-mono` platinum type.
- **Interactive Cursor Follower:** High-precision ring with inverted blend mode (`mix-blend-mode: difference`) that expands when hovering over interactive 3D canvases.
- **Audio-Visual Media Scrubber:** 2px high-voltage cyan progress track paired with an ultraviolet buffered indicator against an obsidian rail.
