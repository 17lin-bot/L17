Frequently Asked Questions / 常見合作諮詢

清楚透明的交件格式、預算架構與製作期程，確保跨國與各型專案流暢運作。

Depending on scope, standalone 3D Key Visuals generally require 1.5 to 3 weeks from initial concept moodboard to final 8K render passes. Full-scale CGI animations (15–60 seconds) or interactive WebGL environments require 4 to 8 weeks, partitioned into Milestone Checkpoints: Styleframe Approval → Greybox Animation → Lighting & Shader Refinement → Final Mastering.

單張高解析度靜態主視覺通常需要 1.5 至 3 週；完整的商業動態影像或 3D WebGL 沉浸式網站則為 4 至 8 週，包含嚴格的階段審核確認。

We operate on fixed-fee project retainers customized to geometry complexity, lighting fidelity, and commercial usage rights. Minimum commission retainers start at $2,500 USD for single concept visuals. Comprehensive commercial brand packages and interactive digital spaces are scoped upon receiving your brief. 50% deposit upon milestone contract signoff, 50% upon final master bundle delivery.

費用依幾何複雜度、著色精細度與商業授權範疇訂定。單一概念美術專案門檻自 2,500 美元起；品牌整案規劃將在檢視 Brief 後提供詳細工時與里程碑報價單。
